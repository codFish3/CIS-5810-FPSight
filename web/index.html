Reading your results

Aim distance
Distance in pixels from the crosshair to the upper reference point of the selected target. Compare it with the replay to review aiming movement.
Crosshair alignment
Percentage of frames with tracked targets where the crosshair is inside a target box. Frames without tracked targets are excluded.
Time to alignment
Time from a continuous target observation to the crosshair entering its box. Targets already aligned at first observation are listed separately. A dash means no eligible event.
Detection score threshold
Only candidates at or above this score are retained. Lower values can show more targets and more unwanted boxes. Scores indicate relative ranking.
Sampling rate
Number of video frames analyzed each second. Higher rates provide more timing detail and take longer to process.
Analysis modes
Standard, High resolution, Small targets and Multi-view offer different detection settings. Compare the boxes and tracked events on your recording. Multi-view takes longer.
