Metric Definitions and Limitations

Aim distance
Euclidean distance in pixels from the crosshair to the anchor 15% down the target box. Normalized distance divides by the image diagonal. The anchor is a geometric proxy, not a detected head.
Crosshair Alignment
Percentage of sampled frames with confirmed targets where the crosshair lies inside at least one confirmed box. Frames without targets are excluded. This does not measure hits or damage.
Acquisition proxy
Time from the first continuous observation of a target to the first crosshair entry into its box. Initially aligned events are marked separately. Observation gaps restart timing; unaligned events remain null.
Crosshair location
HSV and connected components locate a green crosshair near the screen center. Failure falls back to center and records the source per frame. Other colors require configuration.
Current Data Limitations
The supplied Twistzz spectator recording includes x-ray and player UI. Distant, occluded, or edge targets may be missed. The system cannot establish player visibility, team identity, shots, or hits.
